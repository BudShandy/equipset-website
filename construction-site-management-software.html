What should construction management software cover?

Good construction site management software should support the practical work that happens before, during and after a site activity. That includes controlling access, confirming inductions, managing RAMS, carrying out inspections, recording incidents and observations, tracking actions and maintaining equipment and competency records.

EquipSet is designed around those workflows rather than treating compliance as a separate spreadsheet. A worker can sign in at the gate, complete an induction and confirm the relevant RAMS. An operator can scan a QR code on a machine and complete a check. A site manager can record an inspection, attach photographs and assign an action. The office can then see the resulting record.
