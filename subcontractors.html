What you get
The subbie's folder, digitised

No site admin, no modules you'll never open. Just the things a subcontractor actually gets asked for.

Machine Register

Every digger, dumper and teleporter in one list, with its documents attached. When a main contractor asks, you send a PDF — not a photo of a folder.

Machinery Certificates

GA1 thorough examinations, insurance, CE docs — stored against the machine with expiry dates. You'll know it's due before anyone on site does.

Your Crew's Tickets

Safe Pass, CSCS, plant tickets — tracked per operator with the same expiry warnings. No more finding out at the gate that a ticket lapsed.

QR Machine Checks

Stick a QR label on the machine. Your operator scans it and does the daily or weekly check on their phone — signed, photographed, timestamped.

Expiry Warnings

A dashboard that goes amber at 30 days and red at zero — for machines and people. The phone call from the main contractor never comes, because you fixed it first.

Fault Actions

A failed check raises an action automatically — what's wrong, who's fixing it, by when. The machine's history is there when you sell it, too.
