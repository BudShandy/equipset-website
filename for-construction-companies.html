Construction company management software in Ireland

For a growing contractor, the problem is rarely a lack of information. It is having information in too many places. Site managers have paper inspections, office teams have spreadsheets, equipment records sit in folders and subcontractor documents arrive by email.

EquipSet provides a single platform for the operational records that need to move between site and office. It is suitable for contractors managing one project or a portfolio of active sites.

Built for the way construction teams work

The platform is designed around phones at the gate and on site, with management records available from the office. That makes it practical for businesses where work happens away from a desk.
