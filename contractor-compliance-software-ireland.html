Compliance is a workflow, not a folder

Irish construction contractors have to manage a range of safety and competency information. The HSA guidance covers areas including risk assessment, contractor control, site induction, monitoring and corrective action. The practical challenge is keeping the records connected to the right people, sites, activities and equipment.

EquipSet provides the digital record-keeping layer around those workflows. It can help teams organise information, complete site processes and retrieve records without relying on disconnected spreadsheets and paper files.

Built around the contractor's day

Before work starts, teams can organise site information, RAMS and inductions. At the gate, workers can sign in and complete the relevant process. During the job, supervisors can complete inspections, record incidents and assign actions. Equipment operators can scan QR codes to access machine checks. The office can review the resulting records.

EquipSet supports compliance management but does not replace the employer's legal duties, competent persons, safety professionals or statutory certification processes.

Irish construction context

The HSA states that contractors and other organisations need procedures for controlling the safety and health aspects of contractor work, including pre-planning, project-specific risk assessments, communication, induction and monitoring. EquipSet can help organise the records associated with those processes.
