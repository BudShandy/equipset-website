Construction equipment inspection software

Plant and equipment create a constant stream of inspection and certification records. EquipSet gives each asset a digital record that can be accessed at the point of work.

Operators scan the machine, complete the checklist and submit the result. If a check fails, the issue can be turned into an action. Managers can review histories without searching through folders or trying to match handwritten forms to individual machines.

Useful for contractors and plant operators

The same workflow can support construction contractors managing their own fleet as well as businesses that hire equipment and need clearer machine records.
