About EquipSet

Construction software built around the work, not the boardroom.

EquipSet is designed and supported by Techset Computer Solutions in Ireland, with a focus on practical tools that work at the gate, in the yard and on site.

Why EquipSet exists

Construction businesses have no shortage of forms, certificates and information. The challenge is making that information easy to capture, keep current and retrieve when it matters.

EquipSet brings those operational records into one platform. It is built around the people who actually use the system: site managers, H&S teams, operators, subcontractors and office staff.

Built and supported in Ireland

EquipSet is developed by Techset Computer Solutions, an Irish technology business. The platform is designed with Irish construction workflows and commonly used documentation in mind.

Practical software

We aim for software that is straightforward enough to use on a phone at a busy site and structured enough to give management a reliable record. If a feature does not solve a real operational problem, it does not need to be there.
