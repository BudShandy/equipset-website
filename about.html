Who's behind EquipSet
Designed & built by Techset Computer Solutions

Techset Computer Solutions is an Irish technology company. We design and build software and IT solutions for businesses that need tools which actually work on the ground — in the yard, at the gate, and on site — not only on a slide.

EquipSet grew directly out of that approach. Rather than a generic compliance package, it was shaped hands-on with the people who use it: site managers, H&S officers, operators and subcontractors dealing with real equipment, real certs and real deadlines. Every part of the platform earns its place by solving a problem someone on a site actually has.

That's why EquipSet is aligned to Irish practice — HSA guidance, Safe Pass, GA1 examinations and the paperwork main contractors really ask for — and why it keeps growing into a complete site management and safety platform rather than a single-trick app.

We build it, we support it, and we keep improving it. When you talk to us about EquipSet, you're talking to the people who make it.
