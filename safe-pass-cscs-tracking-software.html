Safe Pass and CSCS records in one place

Construction companies need to know whether the people arriving on site have the training and certification information relevant to their work. That can become difficult when records are spread across spreadsheets, email attachments, paper copies and separate contractor files.

EquipSet provides a digital worker record where certification information can be kept alongside site access, induction and attendance records. For subcontractors, the same approach can be used to keep crew tickets organised before workers are sent to a project.

Safe Pass

SOLAS describes Safe Pass as a one-day health and safety awareness programme. The HSA explains that the Construction Regulations 2013 specify the categories of construction personnel to whom Safe Pass or an equivalent applies. Safe Pass registration cards are issued for four years before renewal is required. citeturn0search6turn0search4

EquipSet can store and organise Safe Pass information as part of a worker record. It does not issue Safe Pass cards, deliver the training or determine whether a particular person is legally required to hold one.

CSCS

The Construction Skills Certification Scheme covers specified safety-critical construction tasks. The HSA states that workers carrying out tasks listed in the Fifth Schedule of the Construction Regulations 2013 must complete the relevant CSCS training and hold a CSCS registration card. The listed tasks include occupations such as scaffolding, tower crane operation, slinging/signalling, certain plant operation and roadworks activities. citeturn0search11turn0search10

EquipSet can keep CSCS information against the relevant worker record so site and office teams have a clearer view of the documentation associated with their workforce.

Connect certification with site access

Certification records are more useful when they connect to the actual site workflow. EquipSet's QR-based site induction process can check relevant certification information before a worker is recorded as active on site, while the live attendance record shows who is currently signed in.

Keep the legal responsibility clear

Digital record-keeping supports compliance management, but it does not transfer legal responsibility. Contractors and employers remain responsible for ensuring that workers have the training, instruction, certification and supervision required for the work being carried out.

For official information, see SOLAS Safe Pass and the HSA CSCS guidance.
