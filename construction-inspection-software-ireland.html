Why move construction inspections online?

Inspection records are useful only when they are completed consistently, connected to the right workplace or equipment and followed through when a problem is found. Digital forms make the record available at the point of work and reduce the need to re-enter information later.

The HSA describes active safety monitoring as including documented procedures and activities such as plant or machinery inspections and analysis of safety and health management records. EquipSet provides a digital way to organise those records; the inspection itself remains the responsibility of the appropriate competent person or site team.

From inspection to action

A useful workflow is simple: select the site or asset, complete the relevant checklist, record findings, attach evidence, assign any action and retain the completed record. Managers can then see what remains open instead of searching through paper forms.

Use the same approach for plant

Equipment inspections can be linked directly to machine records. A QR label on the equipment can open the correct record, reducing the chance of a worker checking the wrong asset. Supporting certificates and examination reports can also be stored with the equipment record.
