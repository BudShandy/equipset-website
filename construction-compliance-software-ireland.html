What construction compliance software should help you manage

Construction compliance is not one document or one register. Site teams may need to keep track of workers, site access, training and certification, equipment records, inspections, RAMS and actions arising from checks.

EquipSet brings these records into one construction management platform so that information can be connected to the relevant site, person, machine or activity.

Worker and site records

Keep a digital record of people attending site and connect site induction and certification information with attendance. This can reduce the need to search through separate spreadsheets, paper forms and email threads.

Equipment and inspection records

Keep equipment records, inspection history and actions together. QR-based equipment checks can make it easier for operators to access the correct inspection process from a phone.

RAMS and safety management

RAMS form part of a wider safety-management process. EquipSet can help keep RAMS records and acknowledgements connected with the people and sites they relate to.

Irish construction context

The Health and Safety Authority provides guidance on risk assessment, RAMS and construction safety management. SOLAS administers programmes including Safe Pass and CSCS. EquipSet is a software record-keeping and site-management platform; it does not replace a competent safety professional or the underlying legal duties of an employer or contractor.

For official guidance, see the HSA RAMS and Safe Plan of Action guidance and SOLAS Safe Pass information.
