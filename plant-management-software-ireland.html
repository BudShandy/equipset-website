One record for every machine

Plant teams often need to answer simple questions quickly: What is this machine? Where is it? When was it last checked? Are there outstanding faults? Where is the relevant certificate or examination report?

EquipSet brings those records together. A machine can have a QR code that opens its equipment record, while inspection results, faults, actions and documents remain attached to the same asset.

Useful for contractors and plant hire businesses

Contractors can manage their own fleet alongside site operations. Plant hire businesses can keep machine information and hire activity connected with inspection and documentation records. The same equipment record can be accessed by the people who need it at the site and in the office.

Plant compliance without overclaiming

Equipment requirements vary by machine and use. EquipSet is a record-management platform: it can store inspection results, certificates and examination reports, but it does not determine legal requirements, perform examinations or issue statutory certificates.

The HSA's safety and health management guidance identifies plant or machinery inspections and statutory plant inspections and certification as part of active monitoring. EquipSet can help organise the resulting records.
