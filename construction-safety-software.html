Digital construction H&S management

Construction safety depends on people following processes consistently and managers being able to see where attention is required. EquipSet supports that process by putting the record at the point of work.

Instead of waiting for paper inspection sheets or manually reconciling spreadsheets, site teams can complete forms on a phone, attach photographs and create actions as they go. The result is a more accessible record for the project team and the office.

EquipSet is a management and record-keeping platform. It does not replace the judgement of competent safety professionals or the legal duties of employers, contractors and duty holders.
Frequently asked questions

Questions about construction safety software

What does construction safety software do?

Construction safety software helps teams record inspections, incidents, observations, corrective actions, RAMS acknowledgement and worker compliance in a structured digital record.

Can site teams complete safety inspections on a phone?

Yes. EquipSet is designed for site-based use, allowing teams to complete digital forms, attach photographs and create follow-up actions from the point of work.

Does EquipSet replace a safety professional?

No. EquipSet is a management and record-keeping platform. It does not replace competent safety professionals, risk assessment or the legal duties of employers and duty holders.
