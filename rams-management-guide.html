RAMS guide

RAMS management for Irish construction sites.

A digital RAMS workflow can make it easier to store documents, control versions, record acknowledgement and keep the relevant information with the site and work activity.

What are RAMS?

Risk Assessment Method Statements, commonly called RAMS, bring risk assessment and method-statement information together for a planned work activity. The HSA explains that RAMS can form part of a contractor's safety-management system and should be developed before relevant work begins, with consultation and sign-off by the relevant people.

RAMS are not simply paperwork. The HSA's current guidance describes them as part of planning, briefing, managing and supervising work safely.

A practical RAMS workflow

1. Prepare. Identify the activity, hazards, controls, responsible people and relevant site information.

2. Review. Make sure the RAMS reflects the actual task, location, equipment and people involved.

3. Brief. Workers need to understand the relevant safe system of work before the activity begins.

4. Acknowledge. Keep a record of who has received and acknowledged the relevant RAMS.

5. Review and update. If the work changes or the original plan no longer reflects the task, the RAMS should be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

How EquipSet fits into RAMS management
