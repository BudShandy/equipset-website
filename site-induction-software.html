Construction site sign-in and induction software

Site access is one of the first controls on a construction project. A digital site induction system can make the process easier for workers while giving site management a clearer record of attendance and acknowledgement.

EquipSet connects site sign-in with inductions, certification information and the wider site-management record. That means the same worker record can be used when reviewing attendance, RAMS acknowledgement, incidents or compliance.

Designed for construction sites across Ireland

EquipSet can be used by main contractors, subcontractors and site teams who need a consistent way to manage access and induction across projects.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about site induction software

What is site induction software?

Site induction software digitises the process of registering workers, delivering site-specific induction information, recording acknowledgement and maintaining an attendance record.

Can workers sign in to a construction site using a QR code?

Yes. EquipSet uses QR-based sign-in to let workers access the relevant induction and site process from their phone.

Can EquipSet track who is currently on site?

Yes. EquipSet maintains a live attendance record so site management can see who is signed in and connect that record with worker and compliance information.
