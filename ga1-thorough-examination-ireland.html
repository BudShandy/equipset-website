What is a GA1?

A GA1 is the Health and Safety Authority's report format for recording a thorough examination and testing of lifting equipment under the relevant provisions of the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (General Application) Regulations.

The HSA's GA1 form records information such as the equipment identity, type, serial number, safe working load, tests or thorough examination carried out, defects identified and the latest date for the next examination. The examination is carried out by a competent person. The HSA also states that the GA1 is not an approved or statutory form and that reports may be produced in other formats.

For the current official form and requirements, refer to the HSA GA1 form and the relevant legislation and guidance.

GA1 is not the same as a routine machine check

Construction equipment can have several different types of inspection, examination and maintenance record. A routine operator check or documented weekly inspection is not automatically the same thing as a statutory thorough examination.

For example, HSA guidance for construction plant identifies weekly documented inspections for certain machines and also refers to a certificate of thorough examination being kept on file. The exact requirements depend on the equipment and how it is being used.

This distinction matters when building an equipment register: the machine record should make it clear which documents relate to routine inspections, which relate to thorough examinations, and which relate to other maintenance or certification activity.

What should an equipment record contain?

01

Machine identity

Record the asset, manufacturer, model, serial number and other information needed to identify the equipment.

02

Inspection history

Keep routine inspection results, dates, findings and actions against the correct machine.

03

Examination reports

Store relevant GA1 reports and other supporting documentation with the equipment record.

04

Defects and actions

Record issues found during checks and track the actions needed to address them.

Frequently asked questions

GA1 questions

Who completes a GA1 thorough examination?

The HSA GA1 form is intended to record a thorough examination carried out by a competent person. EquipSet can store the resulting report but does not carry out the examination.

Is a GA1 the same as a weekly plant inspection?

No. Routine inspections and thorough examinations can have different purposes and requirements. The applicable inspection or examination regime depends on the equipment and its use.

Can EquipSet store a GA1 report?

Yes. EquipSet can store relevant equipment documentation against the machine record so the report can be found alongside inspection history and other supporting records.

Does EquipSet issue GA1 certificates?

No. EquipSet is a record-management system. It does not perform examinations or issue GA1 reports.
